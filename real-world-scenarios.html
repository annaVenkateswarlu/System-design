💡 Tips for Solving These Problems

  • Start with requirements - Clarify functional and non-functional needs
  • Think about trade-offs - Every solution has pros and cons
  • Consider scalability - How will your solution handle 10x growth?
  • Don't forget monitoring - How will you know if things are working?
  • Practice explaining - Communication is as important as the solution
